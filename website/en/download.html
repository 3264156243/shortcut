An “unknown publisher” prompt?

The installer is not code-signed. Check that it came from a trusted download link on this page before following the Windows prompt.

Will an update keep my data?

Installing over your existing version keeps shortcuts, tasks and settings. You can also back up important content beforehand.

Is activation needed?

Follow the app prompt to enter a valid activation code. No account is required; contact the administrator through Feedback if you need help.
