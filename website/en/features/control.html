Details

Two connection modes, picked by the situation.

Cloud relay (works on 4G)

Not on the same network: the phone and desktop connect securely and commands are relayed.

Direct LAN connection

On the same Wi-Fi the phone talks directly to the desktop for the lowest latency.

Keyboard input

Chinese text, symbols and everything else your phone keyboard produces is injected into the focused window.

Trackpad and combos

Relative cursor movement, single and double clicks, one-tap modifier combinations.

Clipboard transfer

Send text from your phone to the desktop clipboard, or read it back the other way.

Least privilege

You must enable the control service on the desktop and pair first; you can stop it at any time.
